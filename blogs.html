Understanding Anxiety

What anxiety is, why it happens, and simple techniques to manage it daily.

Anxiety is the body’s natural response to stress. It becomes problematic when it interferes with daily activities.

Common Symptoms

  • Restlessness
  • Racing thoughts
  • Difficulty sleeping

Practicing breathing exercises and grounding techniques can help manage symptoms.

Healthy BMI & Lifestyle

How maintaining a balanced BMI supports long-term health.

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple measurement that helps determine whether your weight is healthy for your height.

BMI Categories

  • Underweight: Below 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25 – 29.9
  • Obese: 30 and above

Why Maintaining Healthy BMI Matters

A balanced BMI reduces the risk of heart disease, diabetes, joint problems, and fatigue. It also improves energy levels and mental well-being.

Simple Lifestyle Tips

  • Eat whole foods and reduce processed sugar
  • Exercise at least 30 minutes daily
  • Stay hydrated
  • Sleep 7–8 hours regularly

Remember, BMI is a guideline — not a diagnosis. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice.

Managing Blood Pressure

Diet, exercise, and habits that help regulate blood pressure.

Blood pressure measures how strongly blood pushes against your artery walls. Maintaining a healthy level is essential for heart health.

Normal Blood Pressure

  • Normal: 120 / 80 mmHg
  • Elevated: 120–129 / less than 80
  • High: 130 / 80 or higher

Why High Blood Pressure Is Dangerous

Uncontrolled blood pressure increases the risk of heart attack, stroke, kidney damage, and vision problems.

Ways to Maintain Healthy BP

  • Reduce salt intake
  • Exercise regularly
  • Limit caffeine and alcohol
  • Practice stress management techniques

Monitoring your blood pressure regularly helps detect early changes and prevents complications.
